# whoisashok.com — Deep-Dive Comic Image Prompts (40 total)

Two images per theory: **Agency Guess** (red panel) and **Physicist Realignment** (cyan panel).
All images are landscape **16:9** (the panels are `aspect-video`). Recommended export: 1280×720 WebP.

## Global style notes (prepend to every prompt if your tool supports it)

- Agency Guess images: flat vector comic style, bright/naive corporate colors, slightly satirical tone. These sit inside a red-tinted glass panel on a near-black site.
- Physicist Realignment images: sleek dark-mode comic style, neon cyan/teal lighting, confident tone. These sit inside a cyan-tinted glass panel on a near-black site.
- Keep important content centered; edges may be slightly cropped by rounded corners.
- No text-heavy images — speech bubbles short, max ~8 words, large lettering (rendered small on mobile).

## File naming convention (so they can be wired in later with zero ambiguity)

`public/comics/<theory-id>-agency.webp` and `public/comics/<theory-id>-physicist.webp`
